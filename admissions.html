Join Our School

Admissions

  1. Submit an Inquiry

    Fill out our online inquiry form and our admissions team will reach out within two business days.

  2. Attend Information Night

    Join us at an upcoming information night to meet our team, see the campus, and learn what makes NCS special.

  3. Complete Your Application

    Submit transcripts, a pastoral reference, and required forms through our secure online portal.

  4. Interview

    Meet with our admissions team for a brief interview as part of the application process.

  5. Enrollment Decision

    Families are notified of admission decisions and next steps for enrollment within two weeks.
