About NCS

A Christian hybrid school — three days on campus, two days at home — built for families who want more than drop-off education.

Our Community

Who We Are

We partner with Christian families to educate children through a biblical worldview, strong academics, and intentional discipleship. Parents are not spectators here — they are partners. That's not a fine print requirement to tolerate; it's the model that makes NCS work, and it's exactly what families who choose us are looking for.

G

Godly Character

Daily devotions, biblical teaching, and intentional discipleship shape students who know what they believe — and why. Christian formation, not just values.

A

Great Academics

A rigorous, well-rounded curriculum from TK through Grade 8. On campus days deliver direct instruction; home days extend learning through teacher-provided lesson plans and guidance.

C

Strong Community

The fellowship of a Christian school with the flexibility of homeschool. Intentional friendships, shared faith, and a community small enough that every family is known.

P

Parent Partnership

This model works best for families who want to be actively involved. Parents volunteer, teach home days, and stay close to their child's learning. That's not a burden — it's the point.

Our Beliefs

What We Stand For

Moral Innocence

We believe children should be allowed to remain children as long as possible. The school does not expose students to moral issues such as sex education or drug awareness — that responsibility belongs to parents at home.

". . . be wise in what is good, and innocent to what is evil."Romans 16:19b
OC

Others-Centered

We believe loving and serving others is not only a command of scripture, but an outflow of salvation.

Tap to read more

Others-Centered

Loving and serving others is both a command of scripture and an outflow of salvation. School life gives children daily opportunities to regard others above themselves and to reflect Christ in how they live.

". . . let each of you regard one another as more important than himself."Philippians 2:3b
